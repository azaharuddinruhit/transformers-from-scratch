    Teaching this to a team: a six-session plan
    Session (60 to 75 min)SectionsThe one thing people should leave with
    1. What and why, , An LLM maps text to a probability for every next token, and text enters as vectors.
    2. Attention, Attention is a weighted average, with weights from query–key dot products.
    3. The model, , A Transformer is one block repeated: attention, MLP, residuals, normalisation.
    4. Training, , , , Training lowers surprise at real text, scale sets how far it can go, post-training turns the result into an assistant, and decoding settings shape what it writes.
    5. Running models, , Memory decides whether a model fits and bandwidth how fast it runs, including for the open models you can run yourself.
    6. The wider picture, , How the original paper relates to today’s models, and what they changed.
    7. Interview practice (optional)The interview drillPairs ask each other questions and give feedback on structure: define, why, how, trade-off, example.

    A format that works: people read the sections and do the checks beforehand; the session opens with the running example, walks through one interactive together on a shared screen, and spends the rest on the “For team discussion” questions.
